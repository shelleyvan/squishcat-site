How it works

  1. Forage

    Go for a walk. Look down. Always ask before you pick.

  2. Press

    Squish it flat in a flower press or under a stack of heavy books. Then wait. The waiting is the hard part.

  3. Create

    Mount it on a collector card, a moon page, or a calendar day worth remembering.

  4. Repeat

    Every season brings something new to find. The colony is always growing.

The Squish Cat Code

A gentle guide for flower friends. It comes with our collector sets, but it's free to live by.

  • Be kind to where you wander.
  • Always ask first.
  • Leave some for the little guys.
  • Stay safe out there.
  • Take only what you need.
  • Respect the wild.
  • Squish responsibly.
  • Be a good human.
